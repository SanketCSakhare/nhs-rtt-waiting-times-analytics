# Operational Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis highlights where elective care recovery teams should focus: specialties with weak 18-week performance, trusts with high long-waiter shares, and regions with persistent backlog pressure.

## Recommended Actions

- Prioritise high-pressure specialties with both low 18-week performance and high 52+ week waits.
- Use targeted validation and booking support for trusts with high long-waiter share.
- Separate productivity actions from clinical capacity actions in recovery planning.
- Monitor month-on-month trend in total backlog and long-waiter share.
- Use specialty-level dashboards to support theatre, outpatient, and diagnostic capacity discussions.

## Measurement Plan

- Track 18-week performance monthly.
- Track 52+ week waits monthly.
- Track median wait by specialty.
- Track recovery-priority trust count by region.
- Review improvement actions with operational owners.
